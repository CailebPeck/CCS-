# Privacy Policy — Coastline Hub

**Effective date: 28 July 2026**

Coastline Hub ("the app") is published by Coastline Current Solutions, 92 Wells St, East Gosford NSW 2250, Australia.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** The app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

- Job selections, booking details (name, phone, address you type in), notes, and price settings are stored **only on your device** and never leave it.
- The app does not use analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDKs that collect data.
- The app does not require an account.

## Product images

Product photos are loaded from our suppliers' public websites. Standard web requests (your IP address) are made to fetch those images, as with any web content. No identifying information is attached.

## Contact and calls

Tapping the phone number opens your phone's dialler; tapping the website link opens your browser. These actions are handled entirely by your device.

## Data deletion

Use **Account → Reset app data** inside the app, or uninstall the app — either removes all locally stored data.

## Changes

If a future version of the app introduces online bookings or accounts, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the app-store data declarations will be updated to match.
